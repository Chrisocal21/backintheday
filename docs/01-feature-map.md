# Back in the Day - Feature Map

Tagline: "Feels like home." Nov 15, 2026. Status: concept locked; sit mode, TV/remote, record player and zone blockouts built (see 03-progress-tracker.md).

## Core Idea
A walkable, first-person nostalgic rec-room in the browser. Cozy hangout, no goals or scores. The room itself is the product. Anyone who walks in should feel at home.

## Platform
- Desktop browser first. Mouse look, keyboard walk, real gamepad support.
- Mobile later, not v1.
- Engine: React Three Fiber (Three.js) on React and Vite. No Unity.

## Room Layout
- Square basement. Staircase along one wall; the space under the stairs is the 70s nook.
- Other three corners: 80s, 90s, early 2000s.
- Shared center: main TV, couch, bean bags, coffee table with the remote.
- Posters live on their own zone's wall, so walking the room reads as a timeline.
- Stairs provide a six-second skippable arrival into the basement. Closed door at the top, light under it, muffled footsteps overhead; upstairs remains a future hook.
- Build one zone at a time.

## Art Style
- Mostly realistic look with a light comic touch: thin outlines and faint halftone in shadows. (Originally a stronger Spider-Verse line-art look; toned down 2026-11-15.)
- Consoles keep true colors and shapes inside that treatment.
- TV screen is the exception: whatever plays on it looks completely normal, a window into real life.

## Likeness Rule (GTA approach)
- Blend real designs into our own. Keep silhouette feel, color schemes, button layouts.
- Invent all brand names. No logos, wordmarks, or real names.

## Sit Mode
- Walk to any chair, press a key to sit.
- Camera locks to seat height. Free look, no walking.
- Each seat has its own angle on the TV.
- Press a key to stand.
- Music and video keep playing while looking around the room.

## TV and Remote
- One TV for both videos and games, like real life.
- Remote sits on the coffee table. Pick it up, it shows in hand.
- Channel up/down flips curated channels, one per era.
- Input button opens a plain on-screen menu: TV / Game, plus a paste-your-own YouTube link slot.
- If a video blocks embedding, TV shows static and "No Signal."
- Curated channel videos are pre-checked to allow embedding.

## Games
- Plug-in consoles and joysticks (Atari-style) with cartridges.
- Pick up controller or joystick, press Input, TV switches to Game.
- Only public domain, homebrew, and freeware titles. No commercial ROMs.
- Keyboard or real gamepad controls.

## Record Player
- Crate of records, sorted by era.
- Music is public domain, Creative Commons, or royalty-free only.
- Separate from the TV. Real songs go through YouTube on the TV.

## V1 Must-Feel-Great Interactions
1. Sit in a chair and look around
2. Pick up the remote and change the TV
3. Drop a needle on a record and hear it play
4. Slide a tape into the VCR
5. Grab a controller and play a game

Everything else (Lego, cards, toys) starts as pick-up-and-inspect objects.

## Zone Item Lists

### 90s (core)
CRT TV with VCR and rental tapes, gray Game Boy-style handheld and Discman, Pokemon-style cards on the floor, Lego pile, Tamagotchi, Furby, slap bracelet, cereal boxes, Goosebumps-style paperbacks, posters (boy band, skate, cartoon or movie), bean bag, lava lamp.

### 80s
Atari-style console with wood grain and joystick, arcade cabinet, boombox with cassettes, Rubik's cube, View-Master, Cabbage Patch-style doll, He-Man-style figures, wood-paneled TV on rolling cart with rabbit ears, posters (neon grid, sports car, movie one-sheet), shag rug, bean bag.

### 70s (under the stairs)
Wood-paneled walls, orange and brown palette, record player with vinyl and floor speakers, Pong-style console with dial controllers, lava lamp, macrame hanging, Lincoln Logs, Etch A Sketch, Magic 8 Ball, papasan chair, corduroy couch, posters (concert, peace sign, sci-fi), console TV with channel dial.

### Early 2000s
Flat-panel or chunky CRT with DVD player and burned discs, GameCube-style and PS2-style consoles, flip phone, iPod-style player, CD binder, bean bag, beaded curtain, inflatable chair, glow stars, Bratz-style dolls, Bop It, posters (pop star, anime or skate, movie), desktop computer with screensaver.
